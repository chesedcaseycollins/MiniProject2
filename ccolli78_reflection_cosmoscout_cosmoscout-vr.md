# Reflection: cosmoscout_cosmoscout-vr

**Pattern.** Declining from a very high start. Development ran at 100 to 280 commits per month from mid-2019 through 2020, then settled to roughly 10 to 100 per month, with smaller peaks in late 2023 and late 2024. 67 authors in WoC. Only 1 gap of 3+ months, and it is at the very beginning: 2019-04 to 2019-06.

**Longest gap.** The commits before it (March 2019) are all logo and icon artwork by Simon Schneegans (":art: Add logo", ":art: Refine logos"). The commits after it (2019-07-15/16) are repository scaffolding: ":tada: Add Contributor License Agreement", "Add initial contribution guide", "Add clang-format file", "Add .gitignore file", "Add external submodules". So the gap is the period between creating the project's identity and preparing the public open-source repository. It is not inactivity in the usual sense and was easy to interpret once the messages were read.

**Recovery.** Immediate and large: 5,431 of the project's 5,442 commits come after the gap. The founder continued and many contributors joined.

**Current status.** Active. The latest GitHub commits (2026-07 to 2026-09, Jonas Gilg) are a main-menu feature PR (#437), a CMake modernisation PR (#439), clang-format passes and a bug fix. Development has slowed since 2020 but has never had another empty month.
